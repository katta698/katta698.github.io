THE STORY

Why · What I learned · How

01 · WHY

54 posts and no way to find them

My blog grew to 54 posts across AWS, Terraform, investing, and life. Finding specific content meant scrolling. I wanted to ask it a question and get an answer.

02 · LEARNED

RAG, embeddings, cosine similarity

RAG lets you answer questions from your own content without fine-tuning any model. Embeddings convert text to numbers that capture meaning. Cosine similarity finds the closest match.

03 · HOW

Two Lambdas, S3, Bedrock, Terraform

An indexer Lambda runs daily via EventBridge. A query Lambda handles questions at runtime. Everything defined in Terraform — no console clicks.
