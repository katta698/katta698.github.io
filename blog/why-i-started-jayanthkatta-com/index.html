Why I Started jayanthkatta.com

Mar 2026 3 min read Originally on Blogger


There comes a moment in life when you realize that learning alone is not enough.

You begin to feel the need to capture the journey—to write down what you are discovering, struggling with, building, and becoming.

That moment is why jayanthkatta.com exists.

This website is not meant to be a perfect collection of polished ideas.
It is a living notebook, a place where I document lessons from technology, health, discipline, fatherhood, and long-term thinking.

Life moves fast. Ideas appear and disappear like clouds.
Writing slows the mind just enough to turn those clouds into something lasting.

A Career Built on Curiosity

My professional journey began in the world of databases and enterprise systems.
Over the years I have worked with technologies like Oracle databases, cloud platforms, and modern data systems. Recently my focus has expanded toward cloud architecture and data engineering—fields that shape how organizations store, process, and learn from massive amounts of data.

Technology evolves quickly. What works today may be obsolete tomorrow.
But the principles behind good systems—clarity, scalability, simplicity, and thoughtful design—remain timeless.

Through this site, I want to share:

  • Lessons from real engineering work

  • Cloud architecture insights (Azure, AWS, data platforms)

  • Practical approaches to solving technical problems

  • My journey from database systems toward cloud and data engineering

Not as a “guru,” but as a builder documenting the path while walking it.

Beyond Technology

A career is only one dimension of a life.

In recent years I’ve become increasingly interested in well-being, habits, discipline, and long-term health. Like many people working in technology, I realized that mental clarity and physical health are foundational to everything else.

This site will also explore topics like:

  • building sustainable habits

  • improving metabolic health

  • developing discipline over motivation

  • balancing work, learning, and family life

These lessons matter just as much as anything learned from code or architecture diagrams.

The Long View

I’ve always been drawn to long-term thinking—the idea that small, consistent steps taken over years can shape a meaningful life.

Technology careers, health, finances, relationships, and personal growth all follow the same principle:

steady progress beats short bursts of intensity.

This website will document experiments in that philosophy—projects, ideas, and reflections that unfold over years rather than weeks.

Writing for the Future

There is another reason this site exists.

One day my daughter will grow up and begin asking questions about life, work, discipline, and purpose.
When that day comes, I want there to be a place where she can see what her father was thinking, learning, building, and struggling with along the way.

Some of the things written here are simply notes for the present.

Some may quietly become letters to the future.

An Ongoing Journey

This website is not a destination.
It is a record of a journey still unfolding.

Some posts will be technical.
Some reflective.
Some practical.
Some experimental.

But all of them share the same purpose:

To think clearly, to learn continuously, and to leave behind a trail of ideas that might help someone else walking a similar path.

If you’ve found your way here, welcome.

Jay
