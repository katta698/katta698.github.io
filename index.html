01 — About

The person behind the posts

I'm an AWS Infrastructure Engineer at an organization mid-migration to the cloud. My focus is building the foundations that teams rely on — networking, IAM, compute, storage — done right, with infrastructure-as-code from day one.

I believe in understanding before doing. This site is a public record of that process: structured learning, hands-on challenges, and honest writeups of what worked and what didn't.

Outside of cloud architecture, I think a lot about Metabolic health, play Cricket for fun, and how compounding works — in systems and in life.
