The three clouds, as they report themselves

Every figure on this page comes from a company’s own SEC filing or its own published region list. Nothing here is an analyst estimate, and where a company does not publish something, the page says so rather than borrowing somebody’s guess.

Microsoft publishes a growth rate, not a figure

“Azure and other cloud services revenue increased 41%.”

Upper bound: Intelligent Cloud, the segment Azure sits inside, reported $137.8B revenue and $57.0B operating income for the year to 2026-06-30. It also contains Windows Server, SQL Server, Visual Studio and Enterprise Services — so it is a ceiling on Azure, not a measure of it, and it is a year where the other two cards are a quarter.

Where one cloud is alone

38 countries have a region from at least one of the three. 19 have all three, 12 have two, and 7 have exactly one — which is a latency and lock-in fact no vendor publishes, because it is a map of where their competitors are not.

  • AWS only — Bahrain, Thailand
  • Azure only — Austria, Denmark, Norway
  • Google Cloud only — Finland, Saudi Arabia

Country names are normalised first: the three spell them differently, and left alone the data treats China and the People’s Republic of China as two countries, and files Taiwan under a third name again.

What this page cannot tell you

  • Market share. Those percentages come from Gartner, IDC, Synergy and Canalys. They are licensed research, not public data, and they are estimates — necessarily so, given the gap above.
  • How many customers each has, or of what kind. None of the three publishes it. Vendors cite selected logos, never totals.
  • Azure’s revenue. It is not that this page has not found it. Microsoft does not publish it. The Intelligent Cloud figure above is the audited segment Azure sits inside — a ceiling, and an annual one. Subtracting your way from it to Azure requires numbers Microsoft does not file either.
  • Market capitalisation is not on this page on purpose. It measures Amazon-the-retailer, Microsoft-including-Office and Alphabet-including-ads. It moves on holiday retail. It says nothing about who leads in cloud.
