How to use the catalog without drowning

  1. Pick a team lane above and open the catalog.
  2. Filter by Type (breakout, chalk talk, workshop...), Level, Role, and Services.
  3. Favorite Must-haves first; keep alternates for the same time slot.
  4. When reserved seating opens, reserve Must sessions in the portal or app - don't rely on favorites alone.
  5. Arrive early onsite; late arrivals can lose a reserved seat.
